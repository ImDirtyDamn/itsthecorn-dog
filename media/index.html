Vintage & sentimental eyewear

They belonged to someone you loved. We bring them back.

Eyeglass restoration is one of our most popular services, because very few optical shops will even attempt it. A grandparent's frames or your own pair from years ago can be repaired, re-straightened and refinished.

The process

Dead → Resurrected

Six stages, as shown in our restoration video. Every pair is fully assessed first, and we'll tell you what we recommend before any work starts.

Assess & tear down

Every frame we receive is fully assessed. Lenses, nosepads, screws and temple tips come off so each part can be treated on its own.

Strip

Old, dull or chipped finishes are stripped back to bare metal, so the new finish bonds properly.

Blast & prep

The surface is blasted and prepped. Bends are straightened and breaks repaired, with welds done under a microscope.

As shown in the video

Color

A durable powder coat in your choice of 1000s of colors. Match the original, or match jewelry, an outfit, even a favorite car.

Cure & finish

The coating is cured and finished. Plastic and acetate frames get a matte or high-luster polish instead.

As shown in the video

Rebuild & lens

New nosepads and temple tips, a full tune-up, and optional custom lenses, including tints and sun lenses.
