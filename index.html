Scene 1 · About the studio

High-budget visuals. Low-budget narrator energy.

ItsTheCornDog Productions makes short orientation and instructional films for workers. The goal is simple: explain how the job works, why each step matters, and keep people watching long enough for it to stick.

Our style pairs polished, cinematic footage with a narrator who takes himself very seriously. The facts are straight. The delivery is deadpan. The jokes land because he doesn't realize he's telling them.

  • Short films built for real workplaces
  • Accurate content, presented with a straight face
  • Just enough humor to keep the room awake
